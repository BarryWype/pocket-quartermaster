One template for every map sheet

Prompt 1, step 2 of the map UI pass. The template on three very different sheets (a smithy, a patrol and a network building), each as a peek and full, far and here. Then the chooser for overlapping markers. For review before any code.

The template, top to bottom. (1) The header: the kind's icon inside its time ring (a difficulty ring for fights), the name, one status line: distance or “You're here”, and time left. (2) What's here, in one line. (3) The main action in the footer, changing with the situation, and a row of secondary actions under it. (4) Tabs in the full sheet, in a fixed order: what the place does, then its order with its progress in the label, then Details. (5) Items as tiles everywhere; tapping one opens its card with its price and its action.

Peek and full. A tap opens the peek: header, summary and main action, sized to its content, with the map still showing above it. Swiping up, tapping More or walking into range opens the full sheet. Swiping down goes back to the peek, then closes. No Close button anywhere: a tap outside, a swipe down and Android back close.

Drawn with the theme's wood, brass and parchment, the game font, the real 44 and 52 pt button heights, and the game's own pixel icons for items, goods, gold and classes. Places keep their emoji, as on the map today.

Smithy

Shows its stock from any distance. Far, the one action is the walk, and its label says how far. Here, the main action is Buy; picking a tile turns it into “Buy that · price”. The order is a tab of its own, after the shop.

Patrol

Foes, the threat with its level and the drops are visible from afar. The fight rules haven't changed, so far the footer says “Walk 320 m to fight”. If you decide heroes may be sent from afar, only that label changes, to “Send heroes”, and the far frames below show where it would go.

Network building

Everything on a building works from afar except collecting, so the main action follows the next useful step: post a hero, then add it to the delivery route, then collect before it's lost. Here, Collect leads. Its full sheet's tabs are what it makes, who works it, and Details.

Chooser for overlapping markers

A tap that hits more than one marker opens a small list anchored to the tap, instead of guessing. One row per marker: its icon in its ring, its name, distance and the same status as its header. Picking one opens its peek. A tap outside closes it. Two markers or more only: a single hit opens its sheet straight away, as today.

What to decide before step 3

  • Send heroes from afar? Today every fight needs the player within 80 m (patrols) or 50 m (places, Sanctum). The patrol mock-ups keep that: “Walk 320 m to fight”. Allowing it is a game rule change for you to make; the template takes either label.
  • What the far “Walk …” button does. Proposed: it centres the map on the place and closes to the peek, so the player sees the way. It never starts navigation.
  • Peek height. Proposed: the peek is as tall as its content, about a third of the screen, so the tapped marker stays visible above it. The map does not pan to keep it in view.
  • Walking into range opens the full sheet only if the peek is the one showing. A full sheet already open stays as it is.
  • Dragging between heights. Proposed: follow the finger on the header only (as the Sheet does today); the body keeps its scroll. If that fights the scroll on Android, More and a tap on the header switch heights instead.
