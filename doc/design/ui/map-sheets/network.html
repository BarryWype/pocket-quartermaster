The network, made easy

Prompt 2, step 1 of the map UI pass. Route mode on the map, the building, site and cart sheets on the template, the network's map markers with their state badges, and the Network tab as a flow picture. For review before any code. The riskiest piece, route mode, also has a clickable prototype: route-prototype.html, beside this file.

The words. “Delivery route” everywhere (never “cart route” or “loop plan”); a stop is a building or place on it; the cart calls at a building and delivers to a place. Every number carries its unit and meaning: “12 of 36 stored”, “Loop 42 min”, “1 risky street”, never “12/36”.

Same example everywhere. A hall in the middle of town; a Farm, a Quarry and a Herb garden; the Crooked Mug (a tavern that wants grain, leaving in 30 minutes), a Smithy (wants stone) and an Apothecary (wants herbs). The cart drives at 3 km/h and carries 100.

Route mode

Opened from the Network tab, the cart's sheet, or “Add to the delivery route” on any building or place (that sheet closes and the stop is added). Everything that can't be a stop is dimmed; the hall, the buildings and the discovered places that want something become large pins. Tapping a pin adds it as the next numbered stop; tapping a numbered stop takes it off. The route redraws along the streets after every change. The slim bar sits above the island menu.

Building

The summary line says the three things that matter: what it makes, how full it is, how often the cart calls. The main action follows the next useful step: post a hero, then add it to the delivery route, then collect when you're there. Tabs: Making, People, Details. The posted hero is a portrait slot; tapping it opens the hero picker.

Site

A site shows its ground and what each building would make there from afar, once discovered; building still needs you there, as today. The Build tab lists the kinds as tiles with their rate and who wants the goods nearby; picking one turns the main action into “Build a Farm · price”.

Cart

The cart's peek says where it is on the loop and what it carries. The main action is the next useful step: plan the delivery route, choose a driver, start, or, when it runs, edit the route. Tabs: Route (the stops in order with what is loaded and delivered), Crew (driver and escort as portrait slots), Last loops.

Map markers and badges

At most one badge per network marker, the most urgent, small enough not to hide the icon and readable at map zoom. Each has its own icon and an accessibility label, so colour is never the only signal. The route line gets direction arrows and opens the cart's sheet when tapped; the cart shows its cargo as one icon. The “Network” toggle in the top bar dims everything else.

The Network tab as a picture

The top keeps the one “next step” line and its button. Under it, the network as a picture: buildings on the left, places on the right, the cart in the middle, a line for each good from where it's made to where it's wanted, badges on the item they concern. Tapping an item opens its sheet on the map; tapping the cart opens route mode. The cards stay below, anything in its last day first.

What to decide before step 2

  • Reordering in the bar. The prototype tries both: long-press a chip and drag it sideways, or tap a chip for “◀ Earlier / Later ▶ / Remove”. If the drag feels right on your phone, the buttons stay as the accessible way; if it fights the scroll, the buttons become the way.
  • Routing through a street. Dragging the drawn line onto another street is hard to get right on a phone map (the map pans under the finger). Proposed: the “Route through…” button, then one tap on the street, as the prototype does. A via point shows as a small brass diamond; tapping it takes it off.
  • Expiring stops. A stop that leaves before the loop ends gets ⏳ on its pin and its chip and a line under the stats. The prototype also shows “gone by then” when the cart would arrive after it leaves.
  • Auto-added stops (step 2). The toast “Farm added to the delivery route · Undo · Edit”, shown above; Edit opens route mode with the new stop highlighted.
  • Badge order. Raid coming, then last day, then full, then nothing collecting it, then no worker.
