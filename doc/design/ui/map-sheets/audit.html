Map sheets, as they are today

Prompt 1, step 1 of the map UI pass. Every sheet that opens from a map tap on feat/supply-lines (at 9b24e24), far (320 m away) and here, with what it answers at a glance, where its main action is, what it hides when far, and what does nothing or says nothing. No code changed.

How these were made. These are drawings from the component code, not screenshots. This session had no simulator or phone to run the debug build on, and the map can't run on the web (MapLibre native, no react-native-web), so there's no way to capture the real screens here. Each frame follows its component line by line: the same blocks in the same order, the English strings from src/i18n/en.ts, the theme's colours and button sizes, and the sheet's real height cap (85% of an 844 pt screen, the body scrolls past that). Example data stands in for the save (412 gold, a three-hero roster). Emoji stand in for the game's icon art. Fonts and exact spacing are close, not pixel-true. The red pins 1 point at the numbered problems listed beside each pair.

Assumed: the network is on (it is on for everyone on this branch), so the time strip and the order show where the code gates them on it.

Why shops hide their contents when far

One commit added it, everywhere at once: fa8cee8, 2 Nov 2026, “prevent showing what each event does when tapping on it if the event is not reachable”. It touched the place, tavern, patrol, place fight, Sanctum, secret and traveller sheets in one go and added the single string “You are too far to see what is here.” No design doc, idea or issue gives a reason, and the commit hides the same way for every kind, so it reads as a blanket “not reachable means not shown” rule rather than a choice made for any one place.

Where hiding still earns its place, because something else in the game depends on not knowing:

  • Secret places before they are entered. The hidden den's and black market's offers stay behind the key (isEntered), not behind distance. That is the real secret, and it must stay. Distance adds nothing to it: the tip already told the player the place exists.
  • Undiscovered network places. “Walk here to discover it” is a game rule (discovery makes the order and the stop exist). The order must stay hidden until discovery; the place's own stock doesn't need to.
  • Undiscovered sites. The ground's quality is learnt by walking there (“You'll see how good it is and what you can build.”). Keep.
  • Traveller rewards. What a traveller gives is a surprise by design (the reward shows only after helping). Their ask (“a healing potion”) isn't a secret and can show from afar.

Everything else (shop stock and prices, recruits, care, the fight's foes and threats, the Sanctum's guardian) has no stated reason to hide, and hiding it makes the player walk 300 m to learn whether the walk was worth it.

Inconsistencies, worst first

  1. HighThe main action is in a different place on every sheet, and is usually not in the footer. Ten of the twenty sheets have nothing in the footer but Close: every place kind, the tavern, the secret place, the site, the building, the cart and the cell. Their actions sit in the body, one per row or card, often below a full screen of order (tavern, smithy). Fight sheets put Fight in the footer; the traveller puts Help there but Trade in the body; the Sanctum swaps Close for Skip during the fight.
  2. HighFar, most sheets show almost nothing. A shop, tavern, fight or Sanctum at 320 m is a title, a distance and “You are too far to see what is here.” The player can't tell whether the walk is worth it. Meanwhile the network order, which has its own walk rule, is shown in full from afar, above that line.
  3. HighThe network order pushes the place's own content down. On the tavern, smithy, apothecary and market, OrderSection comes first: a 64 pt headline tile, a progress bar, three step tiles, a wants list, “Add to the cart's route” and a disabled “Deliver from my bag”. On a 844 pt phone the tavern's first recruit starts below the fold.
  4. High“Send heroes” has nowhere to go yet: every fight needs the player within range. patrolFightProblem and placeFightProblem return tooFar beyond 80 m (patrols) and 50 m (places), and the Sanctum beyond 50 m. Step 4's “Send heroes appears only where heroes can actually go today (fights)” would therefore show nowhere. Sending heroes from afar would be a game rule change; it needs your call before step 4.
  5. MediumLocked actions don't say why in their own label. Fight, Challenge, Buy, Recruit, Build, Deliver, Collect and Heist are greyed with the reason (if any) in a separate grey line, sometimes far below (Fight in the footer, reason in the body), sometimes repeated under every card (the site's four “Walk to the site to build”), sometimes absent (Buy short of gold only turns the price red; the pilgrim's sheet simply has no button when you carry neither a meal nor enough supply).
  6. MediumFive ways to say “walk closer”, and two ways to say how long it stays. “Walk closer to use it (320 m away).”, “Walk closer (320 m)”, “Get within 50 m to recruit (320 m away).”, “Walk to the site to build (320 m).”, “Walk to the Smithy to deliver (320 m).” Time: the strip says “Leaves in 2h 14m”, the line under it says “Leaves in 2 h”, the secret place says “5h 10m left”, and patrols and the Sanctum say nothing (their time is only on the marker).
  7. MediumOne vocabulary, not yet. “the cart's route” (sheets) and “delivery route” (the target word); “Deliver from my bag” and “Hand over” (quests); “Blueprint” and “recipe”; “Close”, “Walk on” and “Skip”; Fight, Challenge, Dive in and Send on a heist for the same act; durations as “15m 00s” on fight sheets (seconds on a quarter hour) and “15m” elsewhere.
  8. MediumAn empty map tap opens a sheet. With the network on, any tap that misses a marker opens the cell sheet (terrain name and a hall button). A near miss on a small marker lands here. The cell sheet's only other use, placing or moving the hall, is rare.
  9. MediumAccessibility labels are missing on most markers. Shops, taverns and secret places only get a spoken label when a flag (unvisited, last day, locked) is set: SanctumMarker passes accessibilityLabel through as undefined otherwise. The cart, the hall, sites without flags, gather spots, quest pins and dig spots have none.
  10. LowThree ways to confirm. The Sanctum asks through a system Alert; the hall move through a centred Panel dialog with plain Buttons (not Wood or Ember); everything else through PartyDialog or not at all.
  11. LowTwo tab styles. Buy · Sell are two compact wood buttons, one dimmed; the network screens use no tabs. Nothing else in the sheets has tabs yet.
  12. LowEmoji mixed with words in strings that reach a Text: the Sanctum warning (“⚠️ One attempt only…”), the secret key lines (“🔒 No mysterious key…”, “🗝️ You have…”, “🔓 Unlocked…”). They come through t(), so the mixed-emoji test, which reads the source, can't see them.
