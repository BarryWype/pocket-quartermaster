# Your supply network · how to play

Once you've beaten the first boss (tier 2), your **supply network** opens. You build things that make goods, places around you want those goods, and a cart carries them between the two. The Network tab, on the Expeditions screen, always says what to do next.

## The pieces

- **Your hall.** Your base. Tap the map, or your own marker, and place it somewhere you'll often be, like home or work. You can move it twice a week.
- **Sites** 🪧 are empty ground on the map. Walk to one to **discover** it: you'll see how good it is, from one to three stars.
- **Buildings.** Standing on a discovered site, **build** one of three:
  - a **farm** makes grain and a little meat, which taverns want;
  - a **quarry** makes stone, which smithies want;
  - a **herb garden** makes herbs, berries and mushrooms, which apothecaries want.

  More stars, more goods. Locals work a building slowly; **post** a hero there for the full rate. A hero whose trade matches (Farmer, Quarryman, Herbalist) does even better.
- **Places with orders.** Taverns, smithies, apothecaries and markets you've walked to each have **one order**: a list of goods, a bar that fills as you deliver, a reward at 25%, 50% and 100%, and a **headline reward** you can't get anywhere else. Every delivery pays gold too.
- **The cart.** Pick its stops: buildings to load at, places to unload at. It goes round them along the streets, again and again: it loads everything it can at a building, unloads whatever a place still wants, and brings the rest home. Give it a driver (a Teamster is best) and escorts; the streets aren't always safe.

## Everything moves on

Places, sites and buildings last **six days**. Every second night, a third of them change, so most of your network holds while a part of it needs a new plan. A ring around each marker drains as its time runs out. In its last day the ring turns orange and gets an hourglass.

When a place leaves, the rewards you reached are yours; the rest of its order is gone. When a building leaves, the stock the cart hasn't fetched is lost and its hero comes home.

## Your first chain

The Network tab walks you through it, one line at a time:

1. Place your hall.
2. Walk to a site to discover it.
3. Build a farm.
4. Walk to a tavern.
5. Set the cart's route: the farm, then the tavern.
6. Make your first delivery (the cart will, or hand it over yourself at the tavern).

## Tips

- **You can always deliver by hand:** at a place, "Deliver from my bag" hands over what it wants from your inventory.
- **Watch the route's danger:** the planner shows the chance of an ambush per loop. A crew that wins keeps the cargo; an empty cart loses it.
- **Raids** are announced a couple of hours ahead (longer with a Watchman posted). Send defenders, empty the building, or let it go.
- **Pick your orders:** two taverns nearby offer different headline rewards. Serve the one you want.
