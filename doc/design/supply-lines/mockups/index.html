The supply network · mockups for review

Step 1 of Claude outputs/supply-network-prompts.md: one page per screen, in the order asked, each at phone width (390) with its empty, normal and full states, the questions it answers at a glance (numbered on the phones) and the decisions to confirm. No screen is built until these are approved. Numbers are placeholders; the layout and wording are what's up for review.

Screens

  1. A site's sheet: undiscovered, discovered, can't build, built.
  2. A building's sheet: just built, running, full with a raid.
  3. A place's order sheet: just discovered, under way, filled.
  4. The route planner: stops (empty and normal), order, result.
  5. The Network tab: empty, normal, full with trouble.
  6. The network on the map: street zoom, zoomed out with the Network filter, legend.

One vocabulary, everywhere

  • Nouns: site, building, place, order, cart, stop, hall. Buildings: farm, quarry, herb garden.
  • Verbs: Discover, Build, Post, Deliver, Collect.
  • Time: "Leaves in 2 days 4 h", in the timer strip under every temporary sheet's header; "LAST DAY" and ⏳ in its last day.
  • Goods: one icon each, everywhere: 🌾 grain (no pixel icon yet), stone, herbs, berries, mushrooms, meat. Counts beside the icon.
  • French: chantier (site), bâtiment, lieu, commande, charrette, étape, hall; Découvrir, Bâtir, Poster, Livrer, Récolter. Ferme, carrière, jardin d'herbes.

Shared rules the mockups follow

  • One ember button per screen: the next useful step. Every other action is wood.
  • Every disabled button has a one-line reason beneath it.
  • Flow, left to right: worker → goods → stock → cart on a building; buildings → cart → places on the Network tab; hall → stops → hall in the planner.
  • Never colour alone: last day = ember + ⏳ + "LAST DAY"; full = gold bar + "FULL"; filled = ✓; not visited = 👣; risky street = hatched + "risky".
  • Two taps from the map to build (site → Build), post (building → Post a hero) or add a stop (building or place → Add to the cart's route).
  • Built from existing pieces: Sheet, Overlay, ParchmentCard, WoodButton, EmberButton, ItemSlot / LootGrid tiles, Price, FillBar, the diamond markers and DifficultyRing.

Biggest decisions to confirm

  • A built site is its building: one marker, one sheet.
  • The planner becomes a guided sheet; the trial's tap-on-map planning mode goes.
  • The order lives at the top of the place's own sheet, above its shop.
  • Rewards are claimed with a tap per step, through the loot grid.
  • The time ring is an outer arc drawn by DifficultyRing, outside any difficulty ring.
  • The Network chip also shows the hall's building reach as a dashed circle.
