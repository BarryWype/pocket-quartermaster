What it must answer at a glance

  1. 1Where am I in planning? The three-step bar.
  2. 2What can I add, and what does each stop give or want? One row per stop, goods by icon, time left on the right.
  3. 3Is a stop about to go? Its time turns ember and bold in its last day.
  4. 4I don't know the best order. "Suggest an order" on top.
  5. 5How do I change the order? Arrows and a drag handle, 44 pt each.
  6. 6What will a loop actually do? The flow strip: ▲ loads, ▼ unloads, by icon and count, and what reaches the hall.
  7. 7How long and how risky? Loop time, length on the streets, ambush chance per loop (danger uses the day's average).
  8. 8What will break? A warning for each stop that leaves before the loop after next ends.

Decisions to confirm

  • The planner is a sheet, not a map mode. The trial's tap-on-map planner goes; the map above shows the result and takes the one "force through this street" tap.
  • The strip uses the cart's capacity and today's stock and wants, so it's an estimate of the next loop; later loops are shown on the cart's sheet.
  • Suggest puts each building before the places that want its goods, then nearest first. It never removes a stop.
  • A stop with nothing to do (a filled order, a building with no stock) stays allowed, greyed in the strip as "nothing".
  • Ambush odds stay per loop in percent, as in the trial; the risky street is drawn hatched and labelled.
