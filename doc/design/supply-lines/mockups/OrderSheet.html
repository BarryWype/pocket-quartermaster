What it must answer at a glance

  1. 1How long is it here? The timer strip, as everywhere.
  2. 2What's the big prize, and is it worth it? The headline tile, large, with what it does and "Only from orders".
  3. 3How far along am I, and what's next? The bar with notches at 25/50/100%, each step's reward tile under it, ✓ when reached.
  4. 4What does it want, how much more, and what does it pay? One row per good by icon, "20 of 40 still needed", gold per unit on the title row.
  5. 5What's already coming? The cart line, and the striped part of the bar.
  6. 6Can I deliver now, and why not? Two actions, each with its reason line.

Decisions to confirm

  • Rewards are claimed by tapping "Claim the 25% reward" (the loot grid opens), not given silently, so each step is a small moment.
  • Two places of one kind show their different headline tiles on the map-side card too (see Network tab), so choosing which to serve doesn't need opening both.
  • A filled order suggests taking the stop off the route; the cart would otherwise unload nothing there.
  • Gold per unit is shown once for the order, not per good, as long as one place pays the same for each good it wants. Say if you want per-good prices.
  • Undiscovered places keep today's sheet with "Walk here to discover it" (as on the site sheet) and no order at all.
